CIDER Lab — Cybersecurity & Intelligent Distributed Edge Research Laboratory

We build practical, affordable AI systems for the edge. Our work spans on‑device LLMs, IRS‑assisted spectrum sharing, federated learning, and robust, privacy‑preserving wireless networks for smart cities and industrial IoT.

Explore Research Areas Publications NSF CRII Project

Overview

CIDER Lab focuses on advancing next‑generation intelligent wireless communication, edge intelligence applications, and affordable AI solutions. We emphasize energy‑aware and secure learning across heterogeneous devices, targeting real‑world deployments and reproducible research artifacts.

  • Affiliation: San Francisco State University (SFSU)
  • Lead: Qun Wang (Assistant Professor)
  • Keywords: Edge AI, On‑device LLMs, IRS, Spectrum Sharing, Federated Learning, PLS, IoT Security

Facilities

  • Testbed: USRP (6 GHz), OpenWRT Wi‑Fi, multi‑panel IRS, sensors
  • Compute: SFSU HPC (A100s), RTX 4090 workstation, Jetson edge nodes
  • Software: PyTorch, NS‑3/5G‑LENA, reproducible open datasets/code

Research Areas

Federated Learning + MPC

Decentralized training with secure aggregation and privacy‑preserving analytics for cross‑organization collaboration.

IRS‑Assisted Spectrum Sensing

Modular CNN/GNN models to boost sensing accuracy and fairness in dynamic 6 GHz Wi‑Fi/NR‑U coexistence.

Generative AI for IoT Security

LLM/GenAI‑driven anomaly detection and rapid response for heterogeneous IoT deployments.

GNN‑Based Physical‑Layer Security

Topology‑aware power allocation and beamforming robust to eavesdropping and interference.

Vision‑based Hand Tracking

Lightweight camera‑only tracking and gesture interfaces for embedded and assistive systems.

On‑Device LLM Applications

Privacy‑preserving assistants for recycling, 3D layout design, and domain‑specific edge reasoning.
